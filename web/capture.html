Capture chess on an n × n board

No man is privileged: a king is a piece that steps one square and can be taken like any other. There is no check and no mate, and the game is won by taking the opponent's last man. That makes three kinds of endgame — one king a side, several kings, or a side with no king at all — and they play quite differently, so they are listed apart. Every material of at most five men is listed, solved or not.
